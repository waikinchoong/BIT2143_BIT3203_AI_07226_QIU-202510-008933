## Working project title

<!-- Max 10 words. Worksheet: "Working project title" -->
EV Charging Station Booking Scheduler

## Target users

<!-- Worksheet: "Target users" — who exactly is affected, and where? -->
EV owners who need to book available charging stations.

## Evidence

<!-- Worksheet: "Why this problem matters (one sentence of evidence/urgency)" —
     expand this into 2-4 sentences with a real, checkable source. A credible source
     beats a confident-sounding claim; add the reference here and in your report later. -->
The number of electric vehicles (EVs) is increasing, creating higher demand for charging stations. Limited charging stations and overlapping bookings may cause waiting time and scheduling conflicts. An intelligent booking scheduler can help allocate charging stations more efficiently.

## Urgency and social value

<!-- Why does this need addressing now? What changes for your target users if this
     problem is solved? -->

Efficient charging station scheduling reduces waiting time, avoids booking conflicts, and improves the charging experience for EV owners. It also encourages the adoption of electric vehicles and supports sustainable transportation.
